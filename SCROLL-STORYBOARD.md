# OneBonsai Gulf: Scroll Studio V2 Storyboard

**Canvas:** contained 16:9 stage on a warm off-white page  
**Scroll:** 500vh, five acts  
**Film:** locked-camera 4K sculpture transformation  
**Typography and UI:** live HTML/CSS, never baked into the film

## Act 0: Introduce · 0–20%

- The architectural bonsai is fully visible on the right.
- Headline: “There is a better way to bring AI into your company.”
- The signal object floats gently and the top progress rail begins.

## Act 1: Connect · 20–40%

- The canopy starts reorganizing and the chartreuse conduits energize.
- Headline: “Start with the business you already have.”
- Copy names ERP, CRM, documents, operations, and human knowledge.

## Act 2: Cultivate · 40–60%

- Ribbons fold toward the core and the sculpture becomes more compact.
- Headline: “Let context take root.”
- Copy moves from signals to context, agents, predictions, and automation.

## Act 3: Intelligence flow · 60–80%

- A dark panel travels up over the light stage.
- Heading: “Intelligence, cultivated.”
- A vertical infographic cascades downward: Existing systems → Governed context → AI action → Business value.
- The three 3D capability cards appear in a restrained intermediate state.

## Act 4: Scale · 80–100%

- Heading changes to “AI becomes infrastructure.”
- Capability cards spring fully into place.
- The scale card turns chartreuse and the flow rail completes.

## Responsive contract

- Desktop/tablet: native scroll scrubbing inside the contained stage.
- Mobile ≤700px: release the pin, show the high-resolution poster once, stack the three narrative acts, then render the full dark flow and capability cards. No video is mounted.
- Reduced motion: hold one representative film frame per act and reduce transitions to near-zero.
