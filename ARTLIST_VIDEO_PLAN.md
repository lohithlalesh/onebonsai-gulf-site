# Artlist video direction

No Artlist generation should begin until the prompts, references, and credit estimate have been confirmed in the browser.

## Approved motion reference

`editorial-connect-systems-higgs-v2.mp4` is the master reference.

- 1920x1080, 24 fps, 4.17 seconds
- Locked three-quarter camera
- Cool soft-gray studio background
- Matte graphite, warm chrome, white light, and restrained electric-lime accents
- One stable central object with four clear surrounding modules
- Motion travels outward from the center, reaches one readable peak, then retraces to the opening state
- No camera movement, cuts, text, logos, particles, smoke, or depth-of-field blur

The seamless loop should be produced in post from one clean forward animation. Generate the forward action, remove duplicate endpoint frames, append the reversed action, and export at exactly 24 fps. This is more reliable than asking the model to invent a perfect loop.

## Shared generation settings

- Use Artlist through the signed-in browser session
- Use the highest-quality image-to-video model available, not a fast draft model
- 16:9 landscape
- Generate one approved start frame before video generation
- Locked camera and fixed focal length
- Generate 4 to 5 seconds of forward motion
- No generated typography
- No logos or recognizable interface screens
- Preserve the same lighting direction and material system across both films
- Export the final palindrome loop as H.264, 1920x1080, exactly 24 fps, muted, and web optimized

## Film 02: Build useful AI solutions

### Visual idea

A compact governed core receives four streams of business information. Inside the core, the streams reorganize into a small set of useful tools: a search lens, an agent node, a prediction arc, and an automation relay. Each tool activates in sequence, then the system returns to its neutral state.

### Artlist prompt

Locked-camera premium 3D product film in a cool soft-gray studio, matching a refined enterprise AI sculpture made from matte graphite, warm brushed chrome, translucent smoked glass, white fiber-optic lines, and restrained electric-lime light. A stable circular intelligence core sits in the center. Four clean data streams enter from separate business modules around it. The core calmly processes the streams and unfolds four distinct functional forms around itself: a precise search lens, a compact AI agent node, a predictive signal arc, and an automation relay. Each form activates in a clear sequence while remaining part of one coherent machine. Motion is controlled, mechanical, elegant, and easy to read. The action reaches one complete activated state and holds briefly. Fixed three-quarter view, no camera motion, no zoom, no cuts, no text, no logos, no humans, no holographic UI, no floating letters, no particles, no smoke, no motion blur, no shallow depth of field. Sharp product visualization, consistent geometry, realistic studio reflections, premium industrial design, 16:9.

### Motion timing

- 0.0 to 0.8 seconds: neutral core and inactive modules
- 0.8 to 2.0 seconds: four inputs flow into the core
- 2.0 to 3.5 seconds: useful tools unfold and activate in sequence
- 3.5 to 4.2 seconds: complete activated system holds
- Post production: reverse the forward action into a seamless 8.2-second loop

## Film 03: Scale AI with control

### Visual idea

One central governance spine anchors a growing network. Identical capability modules duplicate outward in balanced rings while thin guardrails keep every new node aligned. The network grows without becoming chaotic, then returns to one governed unit.

### Artlist prompt

Locked-camera premium 3D product film in the same cool soft-gray studio and the same material language: matte graphite, warm brushed chrome, translucent smoked glass, white light paths, and restrained electric-lime accents. A strong vertical governance spine stands at the center of a low circular platform. Three compact capability modules are connected to it by precise thin rails. The modules duplicate outward in an orderly radial pattern, forming a larger operational network while every new module remains visibly connected to the central governance spine. Fine chrome guardrails extend with the network and electric-lime checkpoints illuminate one by one to show approval, security, and control. The expansion is calm, measured, symmetrical but not decorative, and clearly communicates safe organizational scaling. The action reaches a complete controlled network and holds briefly. Fixed three-quarter view, no camera motion, no zoom, no cuts, no text, no logos, no humans, no holographic UI, no floating letters, no particles, no smoke, no motion blur, no shallow depth of field. Sharp product visualization, consistent geometry, realistic studio reflections, premium industrial design, 16:9.

### Motion timing

- 0.0 to 0.8 seconds: one governed unit
- 0.8 to 2.2 seconds: rails extend and first modules duplicate
- 2.2 to 3.6 seconds: the larger network grows in controlled rings
- 3.6 to 4.2 seconds: checkpoints confirm and the complete network holds
- Post production: reverse the forward action into a seamless 8.2-second loop
