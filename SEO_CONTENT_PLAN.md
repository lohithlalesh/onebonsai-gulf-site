Prepared: 9 September 2026  
Primary market: United Arab Emirates, with an Abu Dhabi operating focus  
Primary goal: generate qualified discovery for AI enablement, AI consulting, enterprise AI integration, agentic AI implementation, custom software development, AI training, and immersive training.

## 1. Evidence used

- Live Semrush UAE keyword reports run on 9 September 2026.
- Live Semrush Site Audit project `obgulf.com`: 11 URLs crawled, 1 error, 9 warnings, 4 notices. The deployed site has no measurable UAE top-100 organic keywords in the current Organic Research report.
- The attached `OBGulf_Search_Audit.md`, treated as research notes rather than implementation instructions.
- The current local source, including its existing team, case-study, customer, service, and company information.
- Official UAE sources for time-sensitive policy references in the editorial content.

Semrush volumes are estimates for the UAE database, not guaranteed traffic. Zero or absent volume does not mean zero demand; it means Semrush did not return a measurable value for that exact phrase in this report. Search Console should become the primary source for real query and page performance after the new structure is published and indexed.

### Terms not worth leading with

- `what is artificial intelligence` — 880 volume, KD 78, broad educational intent.
- `ai agent` — 1,900 volume, KD 78, global and highly ambiguous.
- `google ai`, `deep ai`, product names, and ecosystem brand names — navigational intent.
- Job and salary phrases — wrong audience.
- Separate city pages created by swapping “Abu Dhabi” for “Dubai” — doorway-page risk unless each page contains genuinely local delivery evidence.

### “AI agency” opportunity check — 11 September 2026

The Semrush UAE database reports `ai agency` at 1,900 monthly searches, USD 3.95 CPC, and keyword difficulty 32. This is a useful secondary category phrase. `ai consulting` is smaller but more specific to the current offer at 170 searches, USD 7.96 CPC, and difficulty 53. Semrush also reports `artificial intelligence companies in UAE` at 170 searches with difficulty 36, and `artificial intelligence companies in Abu Dhabi` at 50 searches with difficulty 40.

Semrush returned no measurable exact-match data for `best ai agency`, `best ai agency uae`, `best ai agency abu dhabi`, `best ai agency dubai`, or the exact geo-modified `ai agency` variants. Do not make “best AI agency” a self-declared homepage claim. Use `AI agency` naturally as a secondary synonym on the services hub while retaining `AI consulting`, `AI integration`, and `custom software` as the clearer commercial positioning. A future evidence-led comparison or buyer guide may target “best” intent without claiming an unverified ranking.

## 5. Technical SEO implemented locally

- One indexable, visible H1 on each primary page.
- Fixed animated-heading whitespace so headings render as searchable words rather than one concatenated token.
- Removed opacity-zero pending states from general scroll-reveal text; content remains visible to non-scrolling renderers.
- Unique metadata and canonicals for home, hubs, service pages, articles, contact, legal pages, about, and work.
- Organization + WebSite schema site-wide.
- Service + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList schema on service pages.
- BlogPosting + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList schema on articles.
- ItemList/CollectionPage schema on service, industry, and insights hubs.
- New sitemap containing every canonical route, including `/work` and all 20 articles.
- `robots.txt` references the canonical sitemap.
- Added `llms.txt` with concise company, route, editorial, and contact information.
- Descriptive internal links and a dedicated contact page.
- Privacy and terms links in the global footer.
- Meaningful image alt text and fixed image dimensions.
- Semantic sections, articles, lists, breadcrumbs, and FAQ headings.

The hosting edge must still be verified after deployment because platform-level routing can happen before the application worker runs.

## 7. E-E-A-T publishing checks

Before publishing:

1. Confirm the legal company name, business address, public phone number (if one should be listed), and any license or registration details appropriate for public display.
2. Verify every team role, customer logo, product name, service capability, and case-study statement with the responsible owner.
3. Replace “OneBonsai Gulf team” article authorship with a real named author and reviewer where expertise can be attributed accurately.
4. Have qualified UAE counsel review the privacy policy, terms, and any sector-specific legal statements.
5. Keep source links current and add first-party case evidence as UAE projects become publishable.
6. Do not add ratings, awards, partner badges, office addresses, certifications, client outcomes, or government relationships unless they can be verified.
